# Cyber-Hygiene for non-techies
A beginner's guide to personal cybersecurity
###
## Overview
As you are aware, the online attacks are becoming increasingly common and there is a constant looming fear that you are next. This is becoming a major concern for all the people using internet. Not having basic knowledge of cyberattacks and defense can make a user highly vulnerable. 
###
## 💪 Setting up a Strong Password
There are a few rules for setting a strong unguessable password
- The password should be 10 characters or more
- The password should atleast contain 2 or more special characters
- The password should contain atleast 2 or more digits
- Avoid using plain dictionary words since they can be cracked using just dictionary attack
- Avoid using dates that are special to you like Birth-date and Anniversary etc
- Also avoid common phrases or lyrics from a song
#####
## ⛑️ Precautions for passwords
- Don't write your password down somewhere or store it digitally as a file.
- Don't make one password the Herecules. Use different passwords for different accounts to avoid Credential stuffing ( If a password is leaked, all your accounts will be accessible to attacker)
###
## 💼 Password Manager
Now I know that memorizing a lot of unique, unguessable passwords is a difficult task but fear not for we have a tool for that too. Set up a Password Manager. Now it does not only store passwords, it also generates strong passwords for you and even has autofill feature.
####
## 🔑 2 Factor Authentication (2FA)
Now our second line of defense is the 2 Factor Authentication. Now what it does is even though the attacker has the password, the 2FA will now come into the play. The 2FA is the second lock on your door and it can only be opened by something you have. A common example is recieving an OTP or setting up biometric.
####
Now avoid getting OTP through SMS. This can be intercepted by attacker using SIM-swapping attack and your mobile network provider is tricked into transferring your mobile number to their own SMS
####
If possible use email or special Authenticator Apps to avoid hazards of SIM swapping.
###
## 🎣 Phishing
This is a common technique used by attackers that can even make your 2FA almost useless. Phishing works often by sending e-mails to you that look legitimate but are actually coming from the attacker. The reason they are so hard to detect is they use psychological manipulation and different tactics. The only defense is to stay sharp.
####
Here are indications of a Phishy E-mail
- They heighten emotions like excitement or panic. They either are giving you gifts that are too good to be true or just telling you to change your password because it has been compromised.
- They have unprofessional tone and grammar and spelling mistakes
- They have slightly different domain than original one like Google.com is disguised as Goog1e.com which is very subtle.
####
These are precautionary measures to take:
- If email is from a social media app just visit official website to see if something is actually wrong.
- Never share your password on websites that are giving away free Iphones and stuff.
- If it is a family member, phone them to check if this email was actually sent by them.
###
## ☣️ Public Wi-Fi Hazard
Now I know this is an offer that is tempting. A free Wi-Fi at a cafe or bus but no it is not free. It costs you your security and privacy. The thing is anyone within the range of unencrypted Wifi can sniff the data being sent and possibly password or texts.
####
Prevention 
- Avoid logging into your Bank Account or entering sensitive passwords while connected to Public Wi-Fi
- Use a VPN service when connecting to Public Wi-Fi
- Always forget network after you are done using the Public Wi-Fi
###
Now you have the basics needed to browse internet safely and if you follow these tips, you will be safe and less vulnerable to cyberattacks.
